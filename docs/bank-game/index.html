      Rules
      1. If a bank fails, its lenders lose what they lent it. Losses above 2 and that lender fails too.
      2. At the start of each round you may pull any loan you made. If more than 2 is pulled from one bank in a round, it fails, and all its lenders lose, including those who just pulled. Cash does not refill.
      3. Score: +1 per unit still lent to a surviving bank; 0 for a pulled loan; minus the amount lent to a failed bank; −5 if your bank fails.
      Each round
      1. Listen to the news.
      2. Send one person to one bank you are linked to and ask one yes-or-no question. They answer truthfully or say “no comment”.
      3. Write on your paper slip which loans you pull, if any, and hand it in.
